Investor Self-Certification Form

This form must be completed by all investors before accessing the PropertyScan deal marketplace. It is required under the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Financial Promotion) Order 2005. Please read carefully, tick one category that applies to you, complete the declaration, sign and upload as a PDF.

Important: By completing this form you confirm that you understand the risks of investing in property deals, including illiquidity, capital loss, and the absence of FSCS protection for unregulated investments. PropertyScan does not provide financial or investment advice.
Tick ONE category only. Ticking more than one, or ticking a category that does not apply to you, may constitute a false declaration. If you are unsure which category applies, please seek independent financial advice before proceeding.
CATEGORY A
High Net Worth Investor

I declare that I qualify as a high net worth investor because, in the last financial year, I had:

  • An annual income of not less than £100,000 (excluding pension and property income); OR
  • Net assets (excluding my primary residence, pension entitlements, and life insurance policies) of not less than £250,000.
CATEGORY B
Self-Certified Sophisticated Investor

I declare that I qualify as a self-certified sophisticated investor because I meet at least one of the following criteria:

  • I am a member of a network or syndicate of business angels and have been so for at least the last six months.
  • I have made more than one investment in an unlisted company in the two years prior to completing this form.
  • I am working, or have worked in the two years prior to completing this form, in a professional capacity in the private equity sector or in the provision of finance for small and medium enterprises.
  • I am currently, or have been in the two years prior to completing this form, a director of a company with an annual turnover of at least £1 million.

Declaration

I confirm that the information I have provided above is true and accurate to the best of my knowledge. I understand that the investments available through PropertyScan are not regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority and are not covered by the Financial Services Compensation Scheme (FSCS). I understand that the value of investments can go down as well as up and I may not get back the full amount I invest. I confirm that I am a UK resident aged 18 or over and that I am making this certification voluntarily and of my own free will. I understand that a false declaration may have legal consequences.
